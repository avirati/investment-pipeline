## Product

- Ardent describes its product as enabling creation of copies of any Postgres database in under 6 seconds, letting coding agents test, clean, and migrate data on isolated branches with zero risk to production.[E2]
- Ardent's product is designed to let coding agents test their database code on isolated, near-production-like sandboxes to prevent errors that could impact production databases.[E1][E2][E3]
- Ardent sits as a hosted service that replicates production Postgres databases into read replicas, then creates isolated branches from those replicas, presenting them as full Postgres databases accessible with standard connection URLs.[E1][E3]
- Users can start using Ardent's product for free, including signing up and getting started with isolated Postgres branches without mandatory sales contact.[E1][E2][E4]
- Ardent publishes pricing information with a free Starter tier, a Scale tier at $250 per month plus usage, and Enterprise pricing available with custom plans.[E4]

## Market

- Ardent's product depends on recent developments in logical replication and copy-on-write branching technology implemented by providers like neon, allowing near-instant clones of Postgres databases even at terabyte scale.[E1]
- Zennagents CTO Akshendra Garg publicly stated that Ardent allows their agents to test in seconds with zero drift from production data.[E2]
- Chevron's Head of Data, Abhay Singh, described Ardent as a fundamental shift in how agents and developers work with databases, comparing its importance to Git for code.[E2]
- Openledger CEO Pryce Yebesi said Ardent lets them move as fast as agents write code without worrying about agents working on the database layer.[E2]
